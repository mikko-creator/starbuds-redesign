Vendors Days at Starbuds!

Sep 5, 20171 min read

Exciting things are happening at ALL Starbuds locations. We have a schedule of vendor days

where some of the best companies out there set up in Starbuds talking to our customers

about their products and giving EXCLUSIVE deals.

Here is a list of the current vendors who will be set up over the next few weeks,

make sure you stop by to check them out!

9.6.2017 (2:00 PM - 5:00 PM) Nature's High Edibles @ Starbuds DU

9.7.2017 (12:30 PM - 2:30 PM) CSC @ Starbuds Brighton

(3:00 PM- 6:00 PM) 1906 @ Starbuds Brighton

9.8.2017- (11:00 AM- 3:00 PM) Northern Standard @ Starbuds Brighton

9.8.2017- (2:00 PM - 6:00 PM) Lovia @ Starbuds Commerce City

9.15.2017- (2:00 PM- 5:00 PM) O-Pen Vape @ Starbuds Commerce City

9.15.2017- (1:30 PM - 3:30 PM) Cheeba Chews @ Starbuds Commerce City
